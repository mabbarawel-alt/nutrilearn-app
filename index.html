Automated Malnutrition Data Reporting

Compile and electronically submit community malnutrition records directly to the Municipal Health Office.

How Reporting to Higher-Ups Works:

When health workers record child measurements and improvement milestones, NutriLearn automatically aggregates stunting rates, recovery statistics, and parent education metrics. With one click, an official electronic report is submitted to the Municipal Health Office system without cumbersome manual paperwork, enabling fast evidence-based resource allocation.
